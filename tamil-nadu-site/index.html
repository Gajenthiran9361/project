Travel Guide and Itineraries

3 days · Temple trail

Madurai, then Thanjavur, then Mahabalipuram at sunrise.

4 days · Hills and wildlife

Ooty gardens and toy train, Doddabetta, a Mudumalai safari, then Kodaikanal.

5 days · South coast

Chennai, Mahabalipuram, Rameswaram, Madurai and a Kanyakumari sunrise.

When is the best time to visit?

November to February is cool and dry across most of the state. Hill stations are best from March to June.

What should I wear in temples?

Cover shoulders and knees. Footwear is left outside.

Do I need to book ahead?

Book the Nilgiri toy train, safaris and festival-season hotels at least two weeks ahead.

Madurai

Meenakshi Amman Temple

Overview

Madurai is one of India’s oldest living cities, built around the Meenakshi Amman Temple and its fourteen painted gopurams.

Attractions

Meenakshi Amman Temple · Thirumalai Nayakkar Mahal · Gandhi Memorial Museum

Best time to visit

October to March

Travel tip

Visit the temple early morning and dress modestly.

Ooty

Queen of the Nilgiris

Overview

Ooty sits at 2,240 m in the Nilgiri Hills, with tea estates, eucalyptus groves and a heritage toy train.

Attractions

Nilgiri Mountain Railway · Botanical Garden · Doddabetta Peak

Best time to visit

March to June

Travel tip

Carry a light jacket and book the toy train early.

Mahabalipuram

UNESCO World Heritage Site

Overview

Seventh-century Pallava rock-cut caves, rathas and the Shore Temple stand beside the Bay of Bengal.

Attractions

Shore Temple · Pancha Rathas · Arjuna’s Penance

Best time to visit

November to February

Travel tip

Go at sunrise and wear a hat; shade is scarce.

Kanyakumari

Where Three Seas Meet

Overview

At India’s southern tip the Bay of Bengal, Arabian Sea and Indian Ocean meet. Ferries run to the Vivekananda Rock Memorial.

Attractions

Vivekananda Rock Memorial · Thiruvalluvar Statue · Kumari Amman Temple

Best time to visit

October to March

Travel tip

Take the first ferry to beat the queues.

Thanjavur

Brihadeeswarar Temple

Overview

The Chola capital is home to the UNESCO-listed Brihadeeswarar Temple, bronze casting and Tanjore paintings.

Attractions

Brihadeeswarar Temple · Maratha Palace · Saraswathi Mahal Library

Best time to visit

November to February

Travel tip

Visit at dusk when the vimana is lit.

Mudumalai

Tiger Reserve and Forest

Overview

Mudumalai National Park shelters elephants, gaur, deer and tigers in the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve.

Attractions

Jeep safaris · Elephant camp · Moyar river viewpoint

Best time to visit

February to June
